Community Guidelines

A few simple principles to keep conversations useful and welcoming.

01

Respect each other

People have different experiences, budgets and circumstances. Treat them with respect.

02

Keep it constructive

Focus on useful conversations, practical ideas and solutions.

03

Share responsibly

Be honest about what has worked for you. Avoid presenting guesses as facts.

04

Protect your privacy

Don't share passwords, private contact details or sensitive personal information.
